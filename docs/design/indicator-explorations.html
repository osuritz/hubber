What you're looking at

Two independent axes per row. The 3 px rail on the left is coloured by CI status (green = passing, red = failing, grey = running / no checks). The pill on the right is the aggregate review verdict (Approved / Changes / In review / Awaiting). They move independently — e.g. #2 has a green rail (CI passing) but a red Changes pill.

The setting

The Indicator toggle is the real Appearance setting — Rail (default, roomier titles) or Badge to fall back to today's CIBadgeView circle+glyph. The review pill shows in both modes.

Why the pill is text, not just colour

Click Grayscale. The rail is colour-only, so on its own it would collapse to identical grey bars — the WCAG 1.4.1 failure that #117 fixed. The pill keeps the review state as a word, and CI stays readable in the secondary line (1/1 checks, CI failed), so neither axis depends on colour alone.
