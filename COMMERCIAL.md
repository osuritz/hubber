# Commercial Licensing

Hubber is **dual-licensed**: the open-source [GNU GPL-3.0](LICENSE), or a commercial
license available from the copyright holder.

## When the open-source license is enough

GPL-3.0 lets you use, run, study, modify, and redistribute Hubber **for free** —
including inside a for-profit company. There is **no cost and no commercial license
required** to:

- run Hubber as-is, whether personally or across a company's machines;
- modify it for your own internal use;
- fork it and contribute changes back.

The only condition is that any version you **distribute to others** must also be offered
under GPL-3.0, with its source.

## When you need a commercial license

GPL-3.0's copyleft requires distributed derivatives to stay open source under the same
license. A commercial license removes that obligation. You need one if you want to:

- distribute a **modified or closed-source** version of Hubber;
- **embed** Hubber (or parts of it) in a proprietary product;
- redistribute Hubber under terms other than GPL-3.0; or
- obtain **enterprise support, deployment assistance, or commercial features** (for
  example, MDM-managed fleet deployment).

A commercial license can also carry support and warranty terms, which the open-source
license explicitly does not provide.
